## Brief introduction

Lots of multimodal fusion models have been extensively applied in bearing fault diagnosis. However, they overlook the fusion uncertainty among different modalities and the fine-grained selection of fault information within a single modality. To this end, a novel reliable multimodal diagnosis framework named **macroscopic–microscopic information fusion (MMIF)** is developed.

In MMIF, the microscopic physical embedding selection mechanism is firstly proposed to estimate the weight corresponding to different components of single modality, thereby enhancing the representation of intrinsic fault features. Subsequently, a new macroscopic uncertainty quantification scheme with D-S evidence theory is constructed to adaptively solve the uncertainty probability of each whole modality, achieving the fusion of multi-source monitoring signals. Besides, the joint optimization strategy with local weight regularization and global evidence calibration is designed to guarantee the effectiveness of MMIF framework.

Finally, the experimental results demonstrate on both vibration and current modalities that proposed MMIF outperforms several state-of-the-art diagnosis methods.

## Highlights

- A **microscopic physical embedding selection mechanism** is constructed to evaluate the relative strength of fault information contained in each component within a single modality and adaptively assign weights, thereby enhancing the representation capability for fault features.

- A **macroscopic uncertainty quantification scheme** is proposed to estimate the uncertainty of each modality with respect to fault categories, enabling adaptive modeling of modality weights and enhancing the representation capability of modality uncertainty in D-S evidence fusion, thereby achieving reliable fusion of multimodal information.

- A **joint optimization strategy with local weight regularization and global evidence calibration** is designed to collaboratively optimize microscopic component selection and macroscopic reliable fusion, thereby ensuring the effectiveness of the MMIF framework.
